Affiliate Policy

Last Updated: February 2026

This Affiliate Policy is issued by DCORE SYSTEMS LLP, LLPIN: ACT-9625, GSTIN: 27AAZFD2232J1Z3, Pune, Maharashtra, India.

1. Program Overview

The DCore Systems Affiliate Program allows approved individuals or entities ("Affiliates") to earn commissions by referring new paying clients to DCore Systems LLP.

2. Independent Contractor Status

  • Affiliates act as independent contractors.
  • This program does NOT create partnership, employment, franchise, or agency relationship.
  • Affiliates have no authority to bind DCore Systems LLP.

3. Commission Structure

  • Commission rates are defined separately in official communication or dashboard.
  • DCore Systems LLP reserves the right to modify commission structures at any time without prior notice.
  • Commission applies only to successfully completed and paid transactions.
  • No commission is payable on refunded, cancelled, disputed, fraudulent, or chargeback transactions.
  • Recurring commissions apply only while the referred client remains active and fully paid.

4. Commission Lock & Approval Period

  • All commissions are subject to a minimum 30-day lock period before approval.
  • This period allows verification against fraud, chargebacks, or refund requests.
  • DCore reserves the right to extend review period if fraud is suspected.

5. Minimum Payout Threshold

Affiliate payouts will only be processed once the total approved commission balance reaches a minimum of ₹5,000 (or equivalent in other currency).

6. Payment Terms

  • Payouts are processed periodically as determined by DCore Systems LLP.
  • Affiliates are solely responsible for their own tax compliance.
  • Any transaction fees may be deducted from payouts where applicable.

7. Strict Prohibited Activities

Affiliates must NOT:

  • Engage in spam (email, WhatsApp, SMS, cold automation without consent)
  • Make false income or performance claims
  • Bid on DCore brand keywords (including "DCore Systems", "DCore SaaS", etc.) without written approval
  • Run misleading paid ads impersonating DCore
  • Create fake accounts or self-generate purchases
  • Use self-referral links to earn commission on personal purchases
  • Manipulate tracking systems

8. Self-Referral Prohibition

Affiliates may NOT earn commission on their own purchases, purchases by their immediate family, employees, or businesses under their control.

9. No Resale or White-Label Rights

This affiliate program does NOT grant rights to resell, sublicense, duplicate, reverse engineer, or white-label DCore software.

10. Brand & Trademark Usage

Affiliates may only use officially approved marketing materials. Unauthorized modification of logos, trademarks, messaging, or claims is strictly prohibited.

11. Fraud & Chargeback Protection

  • If a referred client initiates a chargeback, refund dispute, or fraud claim, associated commissions will be reversed immediately.
  • Repeated fraudulent activity may result in permanent termination and forfeiture of unpaid commissions.
  • DCore Systems LLP reserves the right to recover legal costs arising from affiliate fraud or misconduct.

12. Termination Rights

DCore Systems LLP reserves the right to suspend or terminate affiliate participation at its sole discretion for policy violations, reputational harm, or suspected abuse.

13. Limitation of Liability

DCore Systems LLP shall not be liable for indirect, incidental, consequential, or punitive damages arising from participation in the affiliate program.

14. Governing Law & Arbitration

This policy is governed by the laws of India. Exclusive jurisdiction: Pune, Maharashtra. Disputes shall be resolved via arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.
